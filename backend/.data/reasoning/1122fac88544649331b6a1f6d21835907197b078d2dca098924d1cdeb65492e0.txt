### ⚠️ GWRR Concentration Risk

GWRR is simultaneously our greatest opportunity and greatest single point of failure:

- **Upside:** One decision-maker controls 112 properties, 15,117 track miles, $19.9M monthly fuel spend. Success here *is* the reference case that unlocks the entire market.
- **Downside:** If GWRR delays 2 quarters, we lose ~$1.5M from the plan. If they walk, we lose the year — and potentially the company.
- **Mitigation:** OmniTrax signed term sheet provides a second proof point. Vermont adds geographic diversity. We need 2-3 more LOIs by Q2.
